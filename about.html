About Stackly

We help citizens reach government services without the queues, confusion or repeat visits.

WHO WE ARE

A digital facilitation centre built around the citizen

Stackly started as a help desk for people who found government forms hard to follow. Today we guide citizens through certificates, licences, welfare schemes and bill payments, and we stay with each application until the document is in your hands.

We are a private facilitation partner, not a government department. We prepare, verify and submit your application to the right office, then keep you updated at every stage.

  • Applications checked before submission
  • Support in English and Tamil
  • Status updates by SMS and email
  • Paperless delivery or nearby collection

Our mission

To make every public service simple to find, easy to apply for and clear to track, so that no citizen loses a day’s wages to paperwork.

🌱

Our vision

A district where any resident can complete a government application in minutes, in their own language, from any device.

OUR VALUES

What we promise every citizen

🔒

Security first

Documents are encrypted in transit and at rest, and only the officers handling your file can open them.

🔍

Full transparency

Every application shows its stage, the officer’s remarks and the expected date, so you are never left guessing.

🤝

Human support

A trained facilitator is a call or visit away to help with forms, documents and corrections.

⚡

Speed with accuracy

We check every file before it reaches the department, which cuts rejections and repeat visits.

OUR JOURNEY

From one desk to a million applications

2018 · The first centre opens

A small facilitation desk in Salem begins helping neighbours with certificate and ration card applications.

2020 · Moving online

Document upload and SMS status updates launch, so families no longer need to travel for routine services.

2023 · 150 services on one platform

Land records, scholarships, health schemes and utility payments join the catalogue.

2026 · One million applications

Citizens across Tamil Nadu use Stackly to finish government paperwork from home or from a nearby centre.
